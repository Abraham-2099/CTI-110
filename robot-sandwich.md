# The sandwich robot: peanut butter and jelly sandwich

## Assumptions

- The robot is standing at a clean empty countertop, facid the counter.
- The robot has two hands both empty and ampty of any tools.
- on the counter within the robots reach, the following items sit untouched and unopened:
    - One unopened loaf of bread in a plastic bag, twist-tied shut.
    - One unopened jar of peanut butter, with a screw-on lid.
    - One unopened jar of jelly, with a screw-on lid.
    - One butter knife, resting on a folded paper towel.
    - One empty, clean plate.
- The robot can identify "open," "closed," "top," and "bottom" of objects, and can twist, pull, and lift with either hand.
- Nothing else is on the counter. The robot does not need to fetch anything from elsewhere.

## Steps 

1. Pick up the plate with one hand and place it in the center of the counter directly in front of you.
2. Pick up the bag of bread with one hand.
3. Untwist the twist tie at the top of the bread bag with your other hand and set the twist tie down on the counter.
4. Open the bread bag by pulling the two sides of the top opening apart.
5. Reach into the bread bag and remove one slice of bread.
6. Place that slice of bread flat on the plate.
7. Reach into the bread bag and remove a second slice of bread.
8. Place that second slice of bread flat on the plate, next to the first slice, without overlapping it.
9. Close the bread bag by pressing the top opening shut and set it back down on the counter.
10. Pick up the jar of peanut butter with one hand.
11. Grip the lid of the peanut butter jar with your other hand and twist it counterclockwise until the lid separates from the jar.
12. Set the lid down on the counter.
13. Pick up the butter knife from the paper towel.
14. Insert the knife blade into the peanut butter jar until it touches peanut butter, then lift the knife out, keeping peanut butter on the blade.
15. Hold the knife blade flat against the first slice of bread on the plate and move it back and forth across the entire top surface of that slice until the surface is evenly covered in peanut butter.
16. Set the knife down on the paper towel, peanut-butter side up.
17. Screw the lid back onto the peanut butter jar clockwise until it stops turning
18. Pick up the jar of jelly with one hand.
19. Grip the lid of the jelly jar with your other hand and twist it counterclockwise until the lid separates from the jar.
20. Set the lid down on the counter.
21. Pick up the butter knife from the paper towel.
22. Insert the knife blade into the jelly jar until it touches jelly, then lift the knife out, keeping jelly on the blade.
23. Hold the knife blade flat against the second slice of bread on the plate and move it back and forth across the entire top surface of that slice until the surface is evenly covered in jelly.
24. Set the knife down on the paper towel.
25. Screw the lid back onto the jelly jar clockwise until it stops turning, and set the jar back down on the counter.
26. Pick up the slice of bread with peanut butter on it.
27. Turn that slice over so the peanut butter faces downward, and place it directly on top of the slice of bread with jelly on it, so the peanut butter touches the jelly and the two slices are aligned edge to edge.

## done When

Both slices of bread are stacked into a single sandwich on the plate, with peanut butter and jelly touching between the two slices, and the robot's hands are empty.
